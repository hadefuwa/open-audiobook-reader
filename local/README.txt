Audiobook Reader - local/offline copy
1. Double-click start.bat (needs Node.js installed). It opens http://localhost:8765
2. Upload PDF/DOCX/TXT files. They are stored in this browser (IndexedDB), nothing leaves your PC.
3. Use the same browser each time - books and progress live in that browser's storage.
For offline speech pick a voice WITHOUT "[needs internet]" (Windows built-in voices work offline).
